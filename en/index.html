WHAT PDFQUILL NEVER DOES
• No app watermark, ever. Your document comes out clean: no PDFquill branding on it, free or Pro.
• No account, ever. No sign-up, no email, no phone number. Just open it and get to work.
• No documents sent to a server, ever. Nothing leaves your phone. No cloud, no server, not even for OCR.
• An announced target is a guaranteed target. If PDFquill shows 187 KB ✔, the file weighs 187 KB.
• Ads never come between you and your document. Never an ad before a task is done, never on the viewfinder, never on the page.
• No ad imitates the interface, and no dark patterns. An ad looks like an ad. A price is written before the button. No traps, no pre-ticked boxes, no countdowns.

SCAN · FIT · ASSEMBLE · ANNOTATE · SIGN · SEND · CONVERT
• Scan: the viewfinder finds the edges on its own and delivers a crisp Photocopy. Documents, ID cards, books, whiteboards.
• Fit: pick the target (200 KB, 500 KB, 2 MB, 4×6 cm, PDF, JPG, PNG…). The Gauge shows where you stand, the Size diagnosis explains where the weight comes from, the Magnifier compares the text before and after. The Quill check ✔ only appears once the real file is measured, to the byte.
• Assemble: merge several files, put pages back in order, rotate, duplicate, delete.
• Annotate: text, check marks, dates, form filling, a dated Protection watermark, and Redact: on export, the redacted area is removed for good.
• Sign: create My quill once, place it with one gesture. It stays encrypted on your phone.
• Send: before export, the checker verifies size, format, portal-friendly file name, metadata, password and resolution. The Send log keeps track of what you sent, and when.
• Convert: Word, Excel, PowerPoint to PDF and PDF to Word, Excel, PowerPoint, JPG or PNG; scan to editable PDF with the scan’s text and images rebuilt in place.
Also: Pre-flight check of a received file without changing it, ID photo with templates, on-device Text & OCR, encrypted backup, widgets and shortcuts, two-pane layout on tablets.

TARGET CATALOG AND GUIDED FOLDERS
• The Target catalog gathers common limits for nearly 200 countries, by application (visa, scholarship, exam, university, bank, email). Checked entries (Tunisia, Morocco, Algeria, Senegal, Côte d’Ivoire, France) show their “verified on” date; the others are common values, labeled as such. Everything is indicative: check the official instructions. Has a value changed? Tap “Report”.
• A Guided folder lists the items to prepare for an application, each with its own target, your progress and a deadline with a local reminder. You export the whole folder at once.
