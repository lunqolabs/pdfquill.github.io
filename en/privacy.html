PDFquill — Privacy & legal

Updated on 2026-11-23 · Lunqo Labs · com.lunqolabs.pdfquill

What PDFquill does with your data

PDFquill processes your documents only on your phone: scanning, fitting, OCR, annotations, signing and export. No document, page, image, quill, form value or recognized text leaves the device.

There is no account and no document server. No personal data is asked of you, and your age is neither asked nor inferred.

Where your data is

The files you export are written wherever you save them. Drafts, capture originals, quills, presets, guided folders, the Send log and settings stay in the app’s private storage.

In Private mode, the app preview is hidden in recent apps and screenshots are blocked when the phone allows it.

Permissions

Camera (to scan), photos and files (through the system picker), notifications (deadline reminders) and device lock (Private mode) are requested on first use, never before. No other permission: no location, no contacts, no microphone, no calendar.

Third-party services

Advertising: Google AdMob, with your consent (UMP form); non-personalized ads if you decline. Ads appear at the bottom of Home and Library, between pages while reading, after an export or a share, and during text recognition, never while you edit a document.

Usage statistics: Google Analytics for Firebase, anonymous events with no content, values in ranges; can be turned off in Settings.

Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics, technical reports with no file name, path, thumbnail or entered value; can be turned off in Settings.

Purchases: Google Play. No payment details go through PDFquill.

Retention periods

Documents: until you delete them. Trash: 30 days. Send log: 12 months at most. Local technical log: 7 days, never sent automatically.

Your rights

Access and portability: Settings › Export my data. Erasure: Settings › Erase everything. Objection: the switches in Settings. Since your data is on your phone, no request requires contacting us.

To request deletion of usage statistics and crash reports already sent (Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics), email contact@lunqolabs.com: we handle requests within 30 days. Otherwise this data is deleted automatically (statistics: 2 months, crash reports: 90 days).
